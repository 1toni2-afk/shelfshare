1. What ShelfShare is

ShelfShare is a place where people put their books on display so they can swap, give away or sell them to each other. The app connects you and gives you a chat — that is all.

We are not a party to the transactions between you. We do not sell books, do not ship them, do not process payments, do not verify their condition and do not guarantee that anyone keeps their word. The agreement is strictly between the two users.

4. Exchanges and sales between users

You arrange everything: the price, the place, how the book changes hands and any payment. Payments do not go through the app.

A few suggestions that are common sense rather than rules: meet in public places, check the book before handing over money, and keep the conversation in the app's chat so there is a record if something goes wrong.

If a dispute arises, you resolve it between yourselves. We can only step in to the extent that someone breaks the rules above — for example by suspending an account — but we cannot recover money or books.
