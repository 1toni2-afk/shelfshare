Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 September 2026

Werewolf Card Game Companion is a companion app for the Werewolf tabletop party game. The app is designed to work locally on your device, without requiring a user account and without our own backend server.

Information We Store

The app may store information that you enter or generate while using it, including:

  • player names;
  • game setups and role configurations;
  • assigned roles, game history, and group statistics;
  • app settings;
  • Premium purchase status.

This information is stored locally on your device so the app can continue games, restore settings, and display history or statistics. We do not sell this information and we do not transfer it to our own server.

In-App Purchases

The app may include a one-time purchase to unlock Premium features. On Android, payments are processed by Google Play Billing.

We do not collect or store your card details or full payment information. Google may process purchase-related information according to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to display banner ads. AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), IP address, and app usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads and measure ad performance. This data collection and use is governed by Google's privacy policy and by "How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services".

Users in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland may be asked to provide consent for personalized advertising in accordance with applicable law.

Premium users do not see ads.

Multiplayer and Local Network

When using Multiplayer mode, player names, selected roles, and in-game actions (such as night choices and votes) may be transmitted directly between participants' devices over the local Wi-Fi network or via Wi-Fi Direct, using one device (the host) as the coordinator. This data stays on the local network between the devices involved in the session and is not sent to our own server. The app may request local network, nearby devices, and location-related permissions on Android, which are used solely to discover and connect to other players' devices during a game session — not to track your location.

Feedback and Contact

If you choose to contact us or send feedback by email, we may receive the information you voluntarily include in your message, such as your email address, message content, and any technical details you choose to share.

Children's Privacy

The app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not require account creation and does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

Permissions and Third-Party Services

The app may use platform services for:

  • saving settings and game data locally;
  • keeping the screen awake during a live game;
  • processing in-app purchases through Google Play;
  • displaying ads through Google AdMob;
  • discovering and connecting to other players' devices during a Multiplayer session (local network, nearby devices, Bluetooth, and location-related permissions on Android);
  • optionally sharing user-generated content if you choose to use the share feature.

Data Security

Game data and settings are stored locally in the app's storage area on your device. You can delete this data by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published at the same public URL.
