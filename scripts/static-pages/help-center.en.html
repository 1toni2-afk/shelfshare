Frequently asked questions

Swaps
How does a book swap work?

You request a book from someone else's listing (you can offer a book in return), the owner accepts or declines, and then you arrange a meeting over chat. Once you have swapped in person, either of you marks the swap as completed.

What happens to a book after I sell or swap it?

The listing becomes permanently unavailable. If the person who received it wants to list it again, they can do so from the Exchanges/Offers screen ("Add to your library") - the book’s history stays traceable on its detail page, with photos added by each owner.

Why doesn’t one of my books show up under Categories or Similar books?

A book’s genre comes from Open Library or Google Books when it is added - some books have no genre in those sources, especially less common editions.

Prices
How is the "bookshop price" worked out?

When you add a book with an ISBN, we try to find its list price on Google Books. Coverage is partial - not every book has a price there, especially older or Romanian editions.

What does "Fixed price, not negotiable" mean?

If the seller ticks this, buyers can no longer send price offers - the book can only be bought at the listed price.

Trust and safety
What is the trust score?

A 0-100 indicator calculated automatically from your activity in the app: how long you have had the account, whether your email is verified, how many swaps you have completed, the rating you have received, how often you reply and how rarely you cancel requests. It is not identity verification, only a signal about behaviour.

How do I report or block someone?

From the menu in the top-right corner of a conversation, or from a listing’s detail page (the flag icon). Blocking stops messages in both directions.
