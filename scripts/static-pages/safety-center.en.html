Safety centre

A few simple rules to keep ShelfShare swaps pleasant and safe.

1

Meet during the day

Schedule the swap for daylight hours, ideally in the morning or the afternoon.

2

Choose a public place

A café, a bookshop or a shopping centre is safer than someone's home address.

3

Prefer places with CCTV

Areas with security cameras discourage unpleasant behaviour.

4

Don't share personal data

You don't need to give out your home address, national ID number or other sensitive details to make a swap.

5

Check the rating and trust score

A solid history of completed swaps is a good sign before you meet someone.

6

A real profile photo builds trust

Profiles with a photo and a filled-in bio feel safer to other users.

7

Check the book's condition before swapping

Compare the book with the description in the listing before you confirm the swap as completed.

8

Report anything suspicious

You can report or block a user straight from their profile or from the conversation.
